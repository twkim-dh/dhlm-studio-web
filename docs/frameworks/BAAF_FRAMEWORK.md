## Scoring System (100 points total)

### 1. GROWTH (25 points)

| Criteria | Max Points | Scoring |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Revenue Growth (3Y CAGR) | 8 | >30%=8, >20%=6, >10%=4, >0%=2, <0%=0 |
| Earnings Growth (YoY) | 7 | >40%=7, >25%=5, >10%=3, >0%=1, <0%=0 |
| TAM Penetration | 5 | <10%=5, <25%=4, <50%=3, <75%=2, >75%=1 |
| New Market Expansion | 5 | Strong=5, Moderate=3, Minimal=1, None=0 |

### 2. PROFITABILITY (20 points)

| Criteria | Max Points | Scoring |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Gross Margin Trend | 6 | Expanding+high=6, Stable+high=4, Declining=2, Low=0 |
| Operating Margin | 5 | >30%=5, >20%=4, >10%=3, >0%=2, <0%=0 |
| Net Margin vs Industry | 5 | >2x industry=5, >1.5x=4, >1x=3, <1x=1 |
| FCF Margin | 4 | >20%=4, >10%=3, >5%=2, <5%=1 |

### 3. MOAT (20 points)

| Criteria | Max Points | Scoring |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Market Share + Trend | 5 | Dominant+growing=5, Leading=4, Top3=3, Average=1 |
| Switching Costs | 5 | Very High=5, High=4, Moderate=3, Low=1 |
| Network Effects | 4 | Strong=4, Moderate=3, Weak=1, None=0 |
| IP/Tech Advantage | 3 | Category-defining=3, Leading=2, Average=1 |
| Brand Power | 3 | Iconic=3, Strong=2, Average=1 |

### 4. VALUATION (15 points)

| Criteria | Max Points | Scoring |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| PEG Ratio | 5 | <1=5, <1.5=4, <2=3, <3=2, >3=1 |
| P/E vs Historical | 4 | Below avg=4, Near avg=3, Above avg=2, Far above=1 |
| P/E vs Peers | 3 | Below peers=3, Near peers=2, Above=1 |
| DCF Upside | 3 | >20% upside=3, >0%=2, <0%=1 |

### 5. RISK (10 points — lower risk = higher score)

| Criteria | Max Points | Scoring |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Debt/Equity | 3 | <0.3=3, <0.5=2, <1=1, >1=0 |
| Revenue Concentration | 3 | Diversified=3, Moderate=2, Concentrated=1 |
| Regulatory/Geopolitical | 2 | Low=2, Moderate=1, High=0 |
| Competitive Threat | 2 | Minimal=2, Moderate=1, Severe=0 |

### 6. MOMENTUM (10 points)

| Criteria | Max Points | Scoring |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Recent Earnings Surprise | 3 | Beat+raised=3, Beat=2, Met=1, Missed=0 |
| Analyst Consensus Change | 3 | Upgrades=3, Stable=2, Downgrades=1 |
| Insider Activity | 2 | Net buying=2, Neutral=1, Net selling=0 |
| Institutional Flow | 2 | Increasing=2, Stable=1, Decreasing=0 |
