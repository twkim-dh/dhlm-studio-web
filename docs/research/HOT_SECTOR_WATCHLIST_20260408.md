## Scoring Rubric

Each candidate is scored 0-5 on each of the 5 sector selection criteria from HOT_SECTOR_FORMAT §2.1. A candidate is "ready to cover" when total ≥ 18 of 25.

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Catalyst-driven price action | 5 | Sector ETF moved >5% in past 14 trading days |
| Earnings revision divergence | 5 | Analyst estimates diverging meaningfully from broader market |
| Macro narrative connection | 5 | Sector is primary beneficiary or victim of an active macro story |
| Underowned by mainstream coverage | 5 | Bloomberg/CNBC/WSJ are NOT covering it as the headline story this week |
| Reader request volume | 5 | Top 10 most-requested tickers (`/api/request-deepdive`) include sector names |

The "underowned" criterion is the highest-leverage filter. A sector that the entire financial press is already covering does not need DHLM Studio coverage.

## Process Notes

### How This Document Updates

1. **Every Friday during Weekly Review**, the editor opens this file and rescores Tier 1 + Tier 2 candidates using fresh data
2. **Tier 1 candidates that get covered** are marked ✅ COVERED with a date
3. **Tier 2 candidates that move up** are promoted to Tier 1
4. **New candidates** are added to Tier 3 first, then promoted up the tiers as evidence accumulates
5. **Candidates that fail to materialize** (no catalyst, no reader interest) are removed after 4 weeks

### Tactical Top Pick Selection Heuristic

For each Tier 1 candidate, the tactical top pick should:
1. Be one of the 5-7 named stocks already in the sector analysis
2. Have the highest asymmetry score (upside in best case vs downside in worst case)
3. Have a thesis that can be invalidated by a specific named event (so the report is falsifiable)
4. Not be the most-talked-about name in the sector (the consensus pick is rarely the right pick)

### How to Find New Candidates

- **Earnings season:** Read FactSet Earnings Insight weekly for sector-level revision divergences
- **Sector ETF screens:** Sort all 11 S&P 500 sector ETFs by 14-day change; investigate any move >5%
- **Reader requests:** Check `/api/request-deepdive` GET response weekly; new names appearing in top-10 are signals
- **Bond market signals:** High-yield credit spreads diverging by sector indicate stress that equity has not yet priced in
- **Insider buying:** Cluster insider buying within a 4-week window across multiple names in one sector is a strong signal
- **Sell-side calendar:** Watch for sector days hosted by major banks (Morgan Stanley TMT, Goldman Healthcare, etc) — they signal that sell-side believes the sector is in motion
